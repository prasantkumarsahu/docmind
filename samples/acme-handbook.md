# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## Leave Policy
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid leave per calendar year. Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward to the next year. Sick leave is separate and capped at 12 days per year.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department head.

## Expense Reimbursement
Travel expenses must be submitted within 30 days with original receipts. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to INR 1,500 per day.

## Notice Period
Employees in their probation period must serve a 30-day notice period. Confirmed employees must serve 60 days.
